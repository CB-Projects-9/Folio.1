About

How

Start a trip, add photos, and add captions. When you are ready, turn the trip into a video or a collage to share with family and friends.

Why

I started taking trips all over the world and came home with thousands of photos. I wanted to share them with my family, but not by sending thousands of single pictures at once. So I made Folio: a place to gather the photos from a trip into one video or collage, and share it.

Where

Your photos and captions are saved only in this browser, on this device. They are not uploaded anywhere. If you clear this browser's data, your photos and captions are erased. To keep a backup, download or print an export of each trip. Your trips do not appear on your other devices or browsers.

Coming Soon

Choosing which photos to send takes time. The goal is to let you add every photo from a trip, have Folio help pick the best ones, and turn them into a video or collage.
